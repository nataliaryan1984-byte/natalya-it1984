01 / THE STORY

Even a warrior can change her destiny.

Once feared as a ruthless warlord, Xena sets out on a path of redemption. With her loyal companion Gabrielle by her side, she travels a world of kingdoms, monsters and meddling gods, choosing to protect those who cannot protect themselves.

This is more than a story of battles. It is a story of friendship, forgiveness, moral courage, and the people we become when we choose to do better.
